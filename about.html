OUR APPROACH

Every detail has a story.

Victoria Fashion House is a Nigerian women's fashion brand and professional fashion training centre. We create clothing with careful attention to the individual woman wearing it, and teach the hands-on skills that help aspiring designers make their own mark.

For us, fashion is personal. It is also practical: a craft to master, a source of confidence and a route to meaningful work.
